# Design System: SmartAgri Assistant

## Theme & Layout
- **Layout Mode:** Centered layout (`layout="centered"`).
- **Primary Page Title & Branding:** 🌱 SmartAgri Assistant.
- **Color Scheme & Tone:** Clean agricultural green accents, intuitive expanders for guides, and distinct warning boxes (`st.warning`, `st.error`) for safety disclaimers.

## Typography & Presentation
- Clear headers (`st.subheader`) for step-by-step user interaction.
- Structured Markdown cards for weather context, disease ID, treatment, budget list, and organic home remedies.
